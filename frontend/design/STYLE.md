# Style guide

The single, current source for how the site looks. Values (colours, fonts) live only in code, as tokens in `src/app/globals.css`; this file names them and says how they're used. Plans reference this file instead of describing styles; when a plan adds or changes a style, this file is updated in the same change. `Design Mockup.png` sets the overall look only.

## The look

- Playful illustrations, quiet UI. The hand-drawn art and the handwritten headings carry the character; cards, controls and popovers stay clean, light and consistent so they don't compete with them.
- A recurring character: the same hand-drawn kid (dark tousled hair with a top knot, mint-and-white shirt) appears on every page from `md` up, in a pose that fits that page (`kid_*` files in `frontend/art/`).
- Lots of whitespace, large rounded corners, pastel panels, pill-shaped controls.
- Light theme only. Colour is never the only way information is shown.

## Tokens (`globals.css`)

- Ink: `navy` (headings, links, controls), `ink` (body text), `primary` (primary button), `error` (error text).
- Accents: `marker` (highlight behind heading words), `sunny` (selected segment, focus ring on inputs), `leaf`, `sky-blue`, `coral`.
- Score scale, cool → warm: `sky-blue` → `leaf` → `scale-low` → `scale-mid` → `scale-high` (see Score meter).
- Panels: `butter`, `mint`, `sky`.
- Fonts: `font-hand` (Shantell Sans 600) for headings and hand-lettered bits, such as the info "i"; `font-sans` (Nunito) for everything else.

## Rules

- **Borders:** 1px `navy/15` on surfaces, `navy/10` for dividers and row lines. 2px `navy` only on inputs and outlined buttons. No dark or thick borders on surfaces.
- **Corners:** sections `rounded-[2rem]`; boxes inside them and popovers `rounded-2xl`; controls `rounded-full`.
- **Shadows:** none, except popovers (one soft shadow, below). No hard or dark shadows.
- **No decoration on UI chrome:** no tilt, tails or pointers, and no hand-drawn borders. Those belong to the illustrations.
- **Hand-drawn art** comes from the team (see `AGENTS.md`). Decorative images are `aria-hidden` with `alt=""`, hidden below `md` where they would crowd the layout. Every kid is hidden below `md` too, also the ones shown with a page's content (score panel, message box).
- **One head size for the hero kids** (home, rankings, compare, how it works, blog, privacy, about): each kid's face is as wide as the home kid's (`kid_1`, `md:w-[21.5rem] lg:w-[24.5rem] xl:w-[29rem]`), compared side by side at the same breakpoint, chins aligned. Not by eye distance: `kid_1`'s face is turned, so its eyes sit close together for its head, and kids sized to its eye distance came out with heads 10–20 % larger _(human decision, 2026-11-10)_. Hair is drawn differently in each artwork, so compare the faces, not the hair. Widths: `kid_blocks_2` `w-54 lg:w-62 xl:w-72`; `kid_compare` `w-58 lg:w-66 xl:w-78`; `kid_wonder` `w-61 lg:w-69 xl:w-81`; `kid_quiet` `w-52 lg:w-58 xl:w-68`; `kid_wave` `w-46 lg:w-52 xl:w-61`; `kid_notebook` `md:w-[12.625rem] lg:w-[14.375rem] xl:w-[17rem]`. New hero art gets its widths the same way. **Size a kid by its head, never by the whole figure:** the poses differ (lying, sitting), so equal figures give unequal heads. Measure on the image as it's converted (after any trimming), beside `kid_1` at the same breakpoint. The score-panel kids are smaller (head ~75 %). Art with a near-white background (`kid_2`, `kid_lens_*`) sits on white only, never on a pastel panel; where it overlaps a border line it uses `mix-blend-multiply` so the line shows through, plus `brightness-[1.03]` where its background would show as a faint box on white (`kid_lens_*`). Art can "interact" with a card by sitting on its edges: flowers peeking over a top edge, plants growing from a bottom edge, the kid peeking over a top edge.

- **Site icon:** the logo's "H", cut out of `art/Logo.png` (the letter only, without the crayon specks around it) and centred on a square with a 3 % margin: `src/app/icon.png` (256 px, transparent) and `src/app/apple-icon.png` (180 px, on `butter`, since home screens don't show transparency). Legible down to 16 px on light and dark tabs. Cut it again if the logo changes.

## Browser pitfalls

Safari's engine (WebKit) ships later than Playwright's, so some of its bugs pass the main E2E suite (plan 017). The Safari check (`frontend/e2e/safari`) catches the first kind below; the others rely on these rules and their component tests. Avoid these:

- **No table row as an anchor.** Safari ignores `position: relative` on `tr`, `thead` and `tbody` (WebKit bug 240961), so anything `absolute` inside (a stretched link, a popover) attaches to the next positioned ancestor instead: one row's link covers the whole card. Anchor to a cell (`td`/`th`) or outside the table.
- **No stretching inside a button.** Safari 18 doesn't stretch a `<button>`'s content to its height, so a pill inside sticks to the top at its text's height. Give the content its own height (`min-h-9` in a `min-h-11 p-1` button) or center it.
- **No built-in clear button on `type="search"`** where text lines up with a layer behind the input (search filters): it narrows the text area in Safari and Chrome. Hide it (`[&::-webkit-search-cancel-button]:hidden`).
- **`svh`, not `dvh`,** for sizes: `dvh` follows the mobile toolbar, so the layout jumps while scrolling.

## Text

- H1: `font-hand font-semibold text-navy`, `text-[2rem] sm:text-[2.75rem] lg:text-[3.25rem]`. Only the home page's H1 has its key words in `.marker`; other pages' H1s have no highlight. A data title (a video's title on its page) is smaller, `text-[1.375rem] sm:text-2xl lg:text-[1.75rem] leading-tight`, clamped to 2 lines, no marker.
- H2: `font-hand text-2xl font-semibold text-navy sm:text-3xl`.
- Body: `text-ink`, intro `text-lg`. Helper text under a control: `mt-2 text-sm text-ink`.
- Error text: `font-semibold text-error`, in an `aria-live="polite"` element.
- Muted note: `text-sm text-ink/80`, centred.
- Divider in a table (the home rankings card's “Least stimulating” between the two ends): a muted note between two dotted rules (`before:`/`after:` `flex-1 border-t-2 border-dotted border-navy/20`, `gap-2.5`), in a short row (`h-9`) with no row lines, neither its own nor the row above's, and no arrow, so it doesn't look like a row to click _(human decision, 2026-11-02, plan 002)_.
- Numbers in columns (ranks, scores): `tabular-nums`, right-aligned in a fixed-width column, never abbreviated (`16,375`, not `16k`).
- Counts in running text (the dataset's size): bold navy (`font-bold text-navy`), exact with thousands separators, never abbreviated; growing ones are `RollingNumber` (see Growing counts).
- Growth badge (`Gain` in `home/Hero.tsx`; home hero only): what a total gained in the past week (the last 7 days, today included), right after the total in the dataset sentence, a no-break space between them: `inline-block rounded-full bg-sky px-[7px] text-sm leading-5 font-bold whitespace-nowrap text-navy`, raised a little (`align-[0.4em]`), a small solid navy triangle before the number (`Arrow`: inline SVG, `M5 3.2 9 10H1Z` filled with round joins, `h-[0.66em]` on the baseline, `mr-[0.14em]`, `aria-hidden`; drawn because the body font has no arrow and a borrowed ↑ looks different per device). A link to the rankings filtered to what it counts (the Channels tab for channels), named "1,043 videos added in the past week" (`aria-label`); hover: the fill deepens to `sky-blue/25`. Left out when the count is 0. Under the paragraph, a key (`mt-3 text-sm text-ink`, `aria-hidden`, since the links say it): the badge holding only the triangle, then "added in the past week"; left out when both badges are. The pastel blue of the How it works panels, not a new colour; navy on `sky` is ≥ 6 : 1.
- Channel without a name (`ChannelName` in `components/ChannelName.tsx`; wherever a channel's name goes): its ID, labeled so it doesn't read as a name: "Channel ID" (`font-sans text-[0.8em] whitespace-nowrap text-ink`), a space, then the ID (`font-mono text-[0.88em] text-ink`, the device's own code font, no web font), regular weight, breaking anywhere. It sits inside the name's link and keeps its hover; the link's name is "Channel ID UC…". Text-only spots (page title, captions) use `channelLabel`. `ink` is ≥ 7 : 1 on white, `sky` and `mint`.

## Data display

- **Tables:** space between columns as right padding on cells, `pr-4 sm:pr-6` (`pr-4 sm:pr-5` after a narrow right-aligned number column), the same on header, rows and skeleton rows; row lines `navy/10`, header line `navy/15`. A linked row (`LinkedRow`): the name's link covers its cell (`after:absolute after:inset-0` on a `relative` cell, never the row, see Browser pitfalls; name `font-semibold text-navy hover:underline`), and a click anywhere else in the row opens the same page (`cursor-pointer`; not on the row's links and buttons, after selecting text, or with a modifier key) _(human decision, 2026-10-30, plan 017: Safari ignored the `relative` row, so every row opened the last one)_; a link in the row's second line (e.g. a video's channel) sits above it (`relative z-10 hover:text-navy hover:underline`).

- **Score meter** (`ScoreMeter`): the score as a bold navy number, then 5 equal segments (`h-2.5 flex-1 rounded-[3px]`, `gap-1`), `aria-hidden`. Filled segments = `max(1, ceil(score / 20))`; one is always filled as a visual convention, and the number stays the exact value. All filled segments share one colour, set by how many are filled: 1 `sky-blue`, 2 `leaf`, 3 `scale-low`, 4 `scale-mid`, 5 `scale-high`; unfilled `navy/10`. Regular size (rankings): number beside the meter from `sm` up, above it on phones. Compact size (measurement rows): number (`w-8`, right-aligned) always beside a `w-[4.5rem]` meter.

- **Score cell** (`ScoreCell`, rankings): the score as a bold navy number (`text-sm tabular-nums`, centred) on a tile, `h-8 w-10 rounded-lg`, in the score meter's colour for that score (same `filledSegments` rule) at 50 % (`bg-sky-blue/50` … `bg-scale-high/50`); navy on every tile is at least 6.3 : 1. No value: a muted `–` (`text-ink/60`) without a tile, its reason sr-only ("No cuts", "No sound", "Not measured"). A count of 0 is a value: "0" on the `sky-blue/50` tile.

- **Rankings table** (`lg`+): a table-fixed table with the table rules above: rank (`w-16`, bold navy, right-aligned; least stimulating first −1, −2… as on the home card), name (title + second line, as linked rows), Overall (`w-14`), then the 12 measurements (`w-12` each) in their three groups. A `navy/10` line on the left of Overall and of each group's first column. Two header rows: the group names (`text-xs` semibold `ink`, centred over their columns), then the column names set vertically (`[writing-mode:vertical-rl] rotate-180`, `text-xs` semibold navy), each a sort button (`w-10 rounded-full py-2`, `hover:bg-sky`; a ▼ under every name, `navy/30` so each reads as clickable; the selected one `bg-sunny` with a navy ▼/▲; `aria-sort` on its `th`). The column names stick at the top of the screen while the rows scroll (`sticky top-0 z-20 bg-white` on each `th`); the group names scroll away. Their line is the header line drawn as `shadow-[inset_0_-1px_0] shadow-navy/15`, as a collapsed border stays with the table, and looks the same stuck as at rest (no drop shadow). The table's links get `scroll-mt-44` so a focused link never hides under the names. Rows `h-16`, `hover:bg-sky/60`. While the next page loads, the rows stay at `opacity-60` with `aria-busy`.

- **Rankings cards** (below `lg`): an `ol` of rows (`py-3`, `navy/10` lines, `hover:bg-sky/60`), `grid-cols-[3.5rem_1fr]`: the rank (bold navy, right-aligned), then the name lines and, under them (`mt-2`, wrapping, `gap-x-5`), "Overall" and the selected measurement's name (`text-sm`), each followed by its score cell.

- **Rankings page**: the H1 and intro (`text-lg`, `max-w-prose`) at the top left, the "What the measurements mean →" link on its own line under it (`mt-1`); the kid (`kid_blocks_2`, lying on the tummy stacking blocks, flipped with `-scale-x-100` so it faces the page, `w-54 lg:w-62 xl:w-72`, the hero kids' head size, `translate-y-[10%]` so body and blocks rest on the line) at the right, on the rankings card's top edge, hidden below `md`. As on the compare page it's `absolute` at the hero's bottom right, rising above the text where it's taller; the text keeps clear with `md:pr-[15.5rem] lg:pr-[19rem] xl:pr-[21.5rem]`. The card (section card) holds the search and view control, the category chips with the order button at the row's right end (`lg`+), the "Rank by" select and the order button beside it (below `lg`; below `sm` the category and "Rank by" selects side by side, `grid-cols-2 gap-x-3`, both `compact`, with the order button under them, its text aligned with the select's edge via `-ml-3`), the results line (`text-ink`), the table or cards, and the page control.

- **Growing counts** (`RollingNumber`, plan 013; the home hero's videos, channels and their growth badges): the sentence, the badges and the key are there from the first paint; until the counts arrive each number is a skeleton bar as wide as its digits (`animate-pulse rounded bg-gray-200 text-transparent`), so nothing moves. The digits are drawn by CSS (`data-digits` + `before:content-[attr(data-digits)]`), never text, and the totals' bars have sr-only "thousands of" / "hundreds of" beside them, so the page as built reads as a sentence (plan 011, Readers other than people). Then each number counts up from its value a week ago (the badges from 0), all starting together and at one speed, ease-out (the score gauge's motion, `useCountUp` in `lib/motion.ts`): the longest rise takes 1 s, the others less in proportion, but at least 0.3 s (`countUpMs` in `RollingNumber`). Only if the counts can't be had does the sentence say "thousands of", without badges or key. The page asks for the counts every minute while the tab is visible; when one rises, only the digits that change roll up (`duration-900 ease-out`), like an odometer: a strip of 0–9 twice behind a one-digit window (`[clip-path:inset(0)]`, which keeps the text's baseline), so 9 → 0 rolls on upwards and then jumps back unseen. Numbers only go up while a page is open; anything lower shows at the next load. `tabular-nums`, `whitespace-nowrap`. With `prefers-reduced-motion` the numbers change without counting or rolling. The moving digits are `aria-hidden`; an sr-only copy holds the value, with no live region, so nothing is announced each minute.

Under it the score as a `font-hand` navy number (`text-5xl`) with a smaller "/ 100" (`text-xl`), centred under the dial, and the score's info button hanging to its right, outside the centring (`absolute left-full z-10 ml-1`, vertically centred; `z-10` so its popover draws over the measurement rows below _(human decision, 2026-10-30, plan 017)_), so "100 / 100" fits the 192 px column on one line. On first render the needle sweeps from 0 while the number counts up (1 s, ease-out); with `prefers-reduced-motion` both show the final value at once. The dial and the counting number are `aria-hidden`; one sr-only sentence gives the score.

- **Measurement table** (`Measurements`): groups with an H3 (`font-hand text-lg font-semibold text-navy`), then one row per measurement (`min-h-11`, row lines `navy/10`): name (semibold navy) and its info button, then a compact score meter (right).
